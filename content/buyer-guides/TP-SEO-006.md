# How to Verify a Perfume Supplier Before Your First Order

You have received a perfume quotation, but the company name differs from the website’s trading name, the product description does not fully match your chosen SKU, or the payment recipient is another entity. These differences need an explanation before you accept or pay for the order. Match the company, product, quotation and payment records to establish who is supplying what, under which terms.

This guide provides a document-matching workflow for branded wholesale purchases and independent private-label projects. Verification helps you identify and resolve missing information; it is not certification of a supplier or a guarantee of an order’s outcome.

**Quick Verification Summary**

| Check | Core information to match or confirm |
|---|---|
| Supplier identity | Connect the trading name, company, sales contact and quotation entity. |
| Business role | Establish what the supplier does and who is responsible for the proposed supply. |
| Product / SKU | Match the product or project reference, specification, version and buying route. |
| Quote scope | Identify what the price includes, excludes and leaves unconfirmed. |
| Payment entity | Explain the relationship between the seller, invoice entity and payment recipient. |
| Timing stage | Distinguish production, dispatch, transit and delivery, including their starting conditions. |

## Verify who you are actually buying from

Start a record for the proposed order. Keep the supplier’s replies and documents together, using the same product or project reference throughout.

### Connect the trading name, company and contact

Ask for the company name behind the trading name or website, its stated business address and registration jurisdiction, and the contact responsible for your inquiry. Where an appropriate public company register is available, use the supplied details to check that you are looking at the intended entity.

Compare that information with:

- The company or trading name shown on the website.
- The email address, domain and other contact channels being used.
- The entity named on the quotation.
- The proposed seller or invoicing entity.
- The payment recipient identified for the order.

These names may describe different parts of a business arrangement. A trading name can differ from a company name; another entity may be involved in a transaction. Obtain and check the explanation rather than assume the difference is harmless or automatically dishonest.

Company information helps identify the business. Product authenticity, stock availability, brand relationships and the proposed supply require their own supporting information.

### Identify what each document represents

Keep a dated copy of the quotation and note its version or reference. If you receive a revised quote, retain the earlier version and identify what changed.

A quotation describes a proposed supply. An invoice or order confirmation should be checked against the terms you accepted. Payment instructions identify where a payment is requested; a payment receipt records a payment.

If the documents use different names or references, ask which entity is selling to you and how the documents relate to the same order.

## Clarify the supplier’s role in the perfume project

A supplier’s role should explain its responsibilities, not simply sound reassuring.

| Claimed role | What to clarify for your order |
|---|---|
| Brand owner | Which brand is owned, and how does that relate to the products being offered? |
| Authorized distributor | Which brand, territory or sales scope does the claimed authorization cover, and how can that relationship be checked? |
| Manufacturer | Which production activities does the business actually perform, and which are handled elsewhere? |
| Trading company | Which entity sells the goods, and who confirms sourcing, product information and order terms? |
| Sourcing partner | What sourcing work is included, and who will be the seller and transaction contact? |
| Supplier coordinator | Which parties or project stages are coordinated, and who answers questions about each part of the supply? |

One business may perform more than one role. Different roles do not automatically mean better or worse supply. The question is whether the description matches the actual arrangement.

For a custom project, ask who will confirm the fragrance, bottle, decoration and packaging scope, and who will communicate changes. A general statement that a supplier “handles everything” leaves those responsibilities unclear.

TopPerfume works as a **trading and sourcing partner**, supporting supplier coordination. This does not describe TopPerfume as a manufacturer, factory owner or authorized distributor.

### Separate a product listing from a brand relationship

A branded product listing does not prove authorization from the brand. If a supplier claims to be an authorized distributor or official partner, ask for information supporting that specific relationship, including its scope and how it can be checked.

Product images, invoices and batch references serve different purposes and should not be treated as automatic proof of authorization. Record whether the claimed relationship has been supported; an unanswered authorization question alone does not establish that the goods are counterfeit.

## Match the product or project to the quote

Give every proposed order line an exact reference. Compare the product information you selected with the supplier’s quotation and any sample reference.

Check the product name, brand, SKU or supplier code, capacity, format, specification, version and quantity unit. If a supplier uses a different code from the website, ask it to confirm the correspondence in writing.

### Keep branded wholesale and private label separate

For branded wholesale, establish the exact finished product and packaging being offered. For a private-label project, establish the eligible product or configuration and the changes the supplier has accepted.

A low wholesale MOQ for an existing product does not mean that product can carry your logo. A website’s private-label service also does not establish that every branded SKU can be relabelled, repackaged or supplied with a different fragrance.

Ask separately:

- Is this quotation for the existing branded product or an independent private-label project?
- Which product or configuration is eligible for the requested customization?
- Where will the logo appear, and which artwork version is being discussed?
- What packaging and fragrance changes, if any, have been accepted?
- What quantity requirement applies to that specific scope?

## Check what the quotation actually includes

Check one quotation against your intended order before comparing it with another offer. A unit price is difficult to interpret when the product, packaging or customization scope is incomplete.

For each scope item, record **Included**, **Not included** or **Still to confirm**, together with the supplier’s supporting reference. A blank field is not a zero charge or an agreement.

### Quote Verification Table

| Field | Details to match or record | Question if the quote is incomplete |
|---|---|---|
| Product / SKU | Exact product, brand, capacity, specification and version | “Which product reference does this line supply?” |
| Quantity | Units per SKU or version, order unit and applicable MOQ | “Is this quantity counted per product, fragrance, design or another basis?” |
| Unit price | Currency, price per stated unit, quantity basis and subtotal | “Which specification and quantity does this price cover?” |
| Logo | Branded item, position, artwork reference and agreed work | “Is this branding included, separately charged or still awaiting confirmation?” |
| Packaging | Bottle, sprayer, cap, label, carton and other requested components | “Which components are supplied, and which are excluded or unquoted?” |
| Fragrance customization | Accepted scent scope and its project reference | “Does the quote include this fragrance request, or only the existing option?” |
| Sample | Availability, sample type, quantity, sample charge and shipping charge | “What will the sample represent, and what will it cost?” |
| Other project work | Any applicable artwork, setup, tooling or other requested work | “Are there additional charges for this scope?” |
| Freight | Destination, payer, quoted transport scope and exclusions | “What does the freight amount cover, and what remains outside it?” |
| Other charges | Any applicable taxes, handling or transaction-specific amounts | “Which amounts are confirmed, excluded or still unknown?” |
| Dispatch / production timing | Relevant stage, starting condition and estimated timing | “Does this refer to production, dispatch or arrival?” |
| Quote version and terms | Date, reference, validity and linked written conditions | “Which version and terms will the order use?” |

For a bundled price, ask the supplier to describe what the bundle includes. You do not need to invent a price for every component, but you do need to know the agreed supply.

Request explicit confirmation of exclusions too. “Packaging included” is less useful than an identified bottle-and-carton configuration. “Custom fragrance” does not automatically establish formula ownership or exclusivity.

## Separate samples, dispatch, production and delivery

These terms answer different questions and should have separate entries in your order record.

### Confirm the sample’s scope and charges

For TopPerfume, sample availability can be confirmed for the selected product or project. **Samples are charged, and shipping is paid by the buyer.**

Ask for the actual sample and shipping amounts for your request. Do not assume a sample charge will be credited against a later order unless that arrangement is expressly confirmed.

Record whether the sample represents the fragrance alone, a finished retail product or an agreed custom configuration. A sample does not confirm the full bulk order: identify the components and versions it covers, along with any differences still requiring approval. Paying for a sample also does not automatically reserve wholesale stock.

For observation and comparison methods, use the [perfume sample comparison guide](https://topperfume.cn/buyer-guides/compare-perfume-samples-wholesale).

### Record the timeline by stage

| Stage | What your record should establish |
|---|---|
| Production lead time | The work required and the conditions from which its timing starts |
| Dispatch | When the prepared order is expected to leave the supplier |
| Shipping / transit time | The transport estimate for the agreed route and service |
| Arrival / delivery | The expected destination milestone and any conditions affecting it |

TopPerfume’s current stock-order wording is:

**For in-stock orders, dispatch is typically around 7 days after order confirmation.**

This applies to dispatch of confirmed in-stock orders. It is not a seven-day delivery or private-label production commitment.

For independent private-label and custom projects:

**Lead time depends on the customization scope and project requirements.**

Ask what “order confirmation” means for your proposed transaction and which decisions remain outstanding. Buyer-paid freight does not, by itself, define the shipping service, customs arrangements, taxes or other delivery responsibilities.

## Verify payment and transaction records before paying

Compare the payment recipient, seller and invoice entity with the commercial documents. If the names differ, ask how they are related and which entity is responsible for the sale. **Matching names do not guarantee safety; unexplained differences require clarification.**

If payment instructions change suddenly, reconfirm them through a previously verified or independently obtained company contact channel—not only by replying to the message announcing the change. This follows the [FBI’s guidance on business email compromise](https://www.fbi.gov/how-we-can-help-you/common-frauds-and-scams/business-email-compromise).

Keep the dated quotation, invoice or order confirmation, agreed written terms and verified payment instructions under the same order reference. Add the payment receipt when applicable; dispatch and delivery need separate records.

TopPerfume’s payment methods and transaction conditions are negotiated for each order and confirmed in writing. Submitting a website inquiry does not create an order.

## What to do when information does not match

Do not resolve a difference by choosing whichever document seems more convenient. Identify the conflicting fields, ask a precise question and request a clear record of the answer.

Use this sequence:

1. **Name the difference.** Identify both documents or messages, their dates and the fields that disagree.
2. **Ask what each reference means.** The difference may concern identity, scope, version or timing.
3. **Request the applicable terms in writing.** Ask which entity, product, quantity or condition will apply to your order.
4. **Obtain a correction where necessary.** A revised quote should identify the accepted scope and any resulting changes.
5. **Recheck affected fields.** A packaging or fragrance change may require quantity, price, sample and timing confirmation again.
6. **Keep unresolved items open.** Pause the affected approval or payment step if you cannot establish what you would be accepting.

### Turn a mismatch into an answerable question

| Information that does not match | Specific question to send | What to record before proceeding |
|---|---|---|
| Website and quotation use different company names | “Why is the company name different? Which entity will sell to us, and how does it relate to the website’s trading name?” | The explained relationship and the entity identified for the transaction |
| Payment recipient differs from the quotation or invoice | “Who is the selling or contracting entity, who receives the payment, and what is their relationship?” | The explanation and independently reconfirmed payment instructions |
| Product listing is branded wholesale, but a message offers your logo | “Is this the existing wholesale SKU or a separate product eligible for customization? Which reference will the quotation cover?” | An identified product or project and its accepted customization scope |
| MOQ differs between the page and quote | “What exactly does each minimum apply to, and which requirement applies to my proposed SKU or project version?” | The applicable quantity, unit and counting basis |
| A chat message mentions a box that the quote does not identify | “What packaging is included in the quoted price? Please identify anything excluded or awaiting quotation.” | A component description linked to the current quote |
| A sample differs from the quoted size or presentation | “Which parts of the proposed order does this sample represent, and which differences still need approval?” | The sample reference, differences and required follow-up |
| “Seven days” appears without a defined stage | “Does this refer to dispatch of confirmed stock, production or transport? What starts the timing?” | The relevant stage, starting condition and qualified estimate |
| Terms remain scattered across messages | “Which terms are still subject to confirmation, and can the agreed points be linked to this quotation version?” | One current record of accepted and unresolved points |

### Illustrative example: a wholesale quote and a logo request

**Illustrative example only—not a TopPerfume order, customer case or confirmed service offer.**

A buyer receives a quotation for a branded wholesale perfume. Later, a message says a logo request can be discussed, but the quote still identifies the original branded product and contains no branding scope.

The buyer should not interpret that message as approval to customize the quoted SKU.

The next question is: “Does the logo discussion concern this exact product or a separate private-label option? Please identify the eligible product and the branding scope before revising the quote.”

If a separate option is proposed, it needs its own confirmed reference, quantity, price, packaging, sample scope and timing. If eligibility remains unanswered, the logo requirement stays unresolved. The original wholesale quotation has not become a private-label agreement.

This is the value of matching records: it exposes the missing decision without inventing the answer.

### Keep a short unresolved-item record

| Field in question | Conflicting references and dates | Question sent | Supplier reply / supporting reference | Outcome |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Enter the exact field | Identify both sources | State what needs explaining | Link the dated answer or revised document | Record matched, request more information, or pause the decision |

Mark a field “matched” only for the identified scope and supporting reference. If that scope changes, reopen the affected check.

## First-order perfume supplier verification checklist

Before accepting the proposed order, check that you can locate the relevant information—not simply recall a reassuring conversation.

- [ ] **Company identity:** The trading name and company information are identified, with unexplained differences resolved.
- [ ] **Business role:** The supplier’s stated role and responsibilities match the proposed transaction.
- [ ] **Product / SKU:** Product references, capacity, specification, version and order units correspond across the records.
- [ ] **Brand relationship:** Any claimed authorization or official relationship has been addressed separately from the product listing.
- [ ] **MOQ:** The applicable minimum and counting basis are recorded for the selected product or project.
- [ ] **Customization eligibility:** Logo, packaging and fragrance requests have specific answers; branded wholesale eligibility has not been assumed.
- [ ] **Sample:** Availability, supplied scope, charges and the relationship to the proposed order are clear.
- [ ] **Packaging:** Included components and any differences from the sample are identified.
- [ ] **Quote scope:** Included, excluded and unconfirmed items are visible in the current quotation record.
- [ ] **Freight:** The payer, destination, transport scope and outstanding charges or responsibilities are identified.
- [ ] **Dispatch / lead time:** Production, dispatch, transit and delivery have not been confused.
- [ ] **Payment entity:** The recipient and instructions correspond to the explained transaction arrangement; unexplained changes have been reconfirmed.
- [ ] **Written confirmation:** The current quote, agreed terms, revisions and unresolved questions are retained together.

Keep unanswered items visible and assign each a follow-up question before treating the review as complete.

## Frequently asked questions

### How can I check whether a perfume supplier is legitimate?

Identify the company, its contact channels and its role in the proposed supply. Then match the exact product or project to the quotation, written terms and payment recipient. Check specific claims using relevant supporting information and follow up on inconsistencies.

The result is a documented basis for your decision, not a universal legitimacy certificate.

### Does a low MOQ prove a supplier can offer private label?

No. A wholesale minimum may apply only to an existing finished product. Private-label eligibility, logo work, packaging and fragrance scope require separate confirmation for the proposed project.

### What should be included in a perfume supplier quotation?

It should identify the supplier, quotation version, product or project, specifications, quantities, units, price and currency. The included supply, exclusions, outstanding charges, applicable timing and written transaction conditions should also be clear.

Where samples, customization or freight are involved, record their scope and charges explicitly.

### Should the payment company name match the quotation or invoice?

Compare the names and establish the relationship between the seller, invoicing entity and payment recipient. If they differ, obtain a specific explanation and verify the instructions through an independently checked contact channel before paying.

### What should I do if supplier information does not match?

Identify the conflicting fields and their source documents. Ask which information applies to your order, request a corrected or clearly linked written record, and recheck any affected quantity, price, scope or timing.

If a material difference remains unresolved, pause the related approval or payment decision.

### Does a sample confirm the bulk order?

Only the identified sample and the parts it represents have been available for your review. Link its reference to the proposed specification and record any differences, components or order conditions that still need confirmation.
